❤️ For Aroosh
Hello, Aroosh

This little book is for you.

Dear Aroosh,

You are growing up every day.

Every day you learn something new — about school, friends, numbers, languages, the world, and yourself.

Mum and Dad are learning too.

So let's keep learning together.

Lesson 1

Life is precious.

Here is one little idea I want you to remember for your whole life.

Life > Time
Life is more important than time.
English: greater than German: größer als

Time is important. We should learn to be on time, keep promises, and respect other people's time.

But sometimes you may be in a hurry. Maybe you are late. Maybe you want to win. Maybe something looks exciting.

Stop and remember:

YOU > EVERYTHING
Your life and your safety come first.
Lesson 2

Take care of yourself.

Before you can take care of the world, you need to take care of yourself.

Your body is your home. Your mind is precious. Your life is a gift.

🌱 Taking care of yourself means…
😴 Get enough sleep.
🥣 Eat properly and give your body energy.
🧠 Look after your mind and your feelings.
❤️ Remember that you are valuable.
🛑 Stop when something is dangerous.
🙋 Ask a trusted grown-up for help.

Loving yourself is not being selfish.

Loving yourself means knowing that your life is beautiful and worth protecting.

Lesson 3

Be curious. Be careful.

You are curious. That's wonderful. Curiosity helps us learn.

But sometimes curiosity needs a little friend: carefulness.

🛡️ When something could hurt you…

🚦
Crossing the road?
Don't hurry because you are late. Stop, look, listen, and cross safely.
🏔️
Somewhere high?
Don't jump down just because it looks fun. Your life is more important than the jump.
🧪
Chemicals, medicines, or poisons?
Never touch or taste something dangerous. Ask a grown-up first.

Nothing is more important than keeping yourself safe.

Lesson 4

The mystery of your lateness 😂

You wake up early.

And somehow… you still become late.

We have tried many things!

🥣 The Oats Mystery

Dad discovered that sometimes you take a very, very long time eating your oats.

“Maybe Dad should use a spoon.” 😂

And somehow our timing became a little better.

One day you will probably laugh about this.

When Mum and Dad are strict about being on time, it is because we are trying to teach you something useful.

Respect time. But never put being on time above being safe.

Lesson 5

Be a Mitmacher!

Your teacher told us that sometimes you seem to be somewhere in another world.

Dad actually likes that you have a big imagination. Your own little world can be wonderful.

But when you are in school / Schule, I want you to be an active:

Mitmacher
Someone who takes part and gets involved.
👀 Pay attention 🙋 Ask questions ✋ Take part 🧠 Think 💪 Try 🔄 Try again

Don't just watch the world happen.

Be part of it.

And keep learning English too. German and English together will open many doors for you.

Lesson 6

Keep trying.

You recently got a 1 in Maths and a 1 in German.

We are very proud of the work you have done.

And now your Sachtest is coming. You told us you are going to get a 1. Let's see! 😊

1
Nice work, Aroosh!
A good result is something to be proud of.

But don't let a good result make you stop trying.

Sometimes things will be easy. Sometimes they will be difficult.

When something is easy, stay curious. When something is difficult, don't give up.

Ask questions. Listen carefully. Practice. Make mistakes. Try again.

We don't need you to be the best. We want you to become better than you were yesterday.

Lesson 7

Your brain needs sleep.

Your body and brain work hard every day. They need time to recharge.

~10
hours of sleep to help you have energy for the day

But then, just when we think you are finally going to sleep…

“Ekta last kotha…” 😂

And somehow the “last kotha” becomes five more conversations.

Dad loves hearing your stories.

Just maybe… start your last kotha a little earlier. 😄

The most important lesson

Love yourself.

❤️

You are important.

Your life is beautiful.

Your body is precious.

Your mind is precious.

Your future is precious.

You are precious to us.

Take care of yourself.

Be kind to yourself when you make mistakes. Ask for help when you need it. Learn from things that don't go well.

And never think that you have to be perfect to be loved.

You don't.

We love you simply because you are our Aroosh.

Remember this

Life is beautiful.

LIFE > TIME

Use your time wisely.

Learn. Play. Laugh. Be curious. Be kind.

And most importantly: take care of yourself.

Your life is more valuable than a clock, a grade, a game, a jump, being first, being fast, or anything else.

Your life comes first.

❤️

Whenever you forget how important you are, remember that Mum and Dad are always here to remind you.

With all our love,
