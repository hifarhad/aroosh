Aroosh's Little Story

Aroosh and the Fastest Ball ⚽

🌤️🏟️⚽
One sunny afternoon, Aroosh went to the football field. The grass was green, the sky was blue, and one football was waiting for him.
“Today, I'm going to score!”
👦⚽💨
Aroosh ran toward the ball. He took one step... Then another... And then—
⚽💥💨💨💨
BOOOOM!
Aroosh kicked the ball so fast that everyone shouted,
“WOW! What a shot!”
⚽🥅
But then the game changed. “Aroosh, we need a goalkeeper!” Aroosh smiled. He put on his gloves and stood in front of the goal.
👦🧤🥅
“I'm ready!”
He watched the ball. He didn't look away. He waited... and waited...
⚽💨💨➡️🥅
Suddenly— the ball came flying straight toward the goal!
🧤💥⚽
“SAVE!”
Aroosh caught the ball. Everyone cheered. But Aroosh didn't shout that he was the best. He simply smiled.
👦⚽❤️
Because Aroosh knew something: Sometimes you score. Sometimes you save. Sometimes you win. Sometimes you learn. And sometimes you just have fun with your friends.
A great footballer doesn't only play with his feet.

He watches. He listens. He thinks. He helps his team.

And most importantly...
❤️ He enjoys the game.

So Aroosh picked up the ball, smiled at his friends,

and said:

“One more game?” ⚽😄

School & Schule

Be a Mitmacher! 🎒

School is not only about getting answers right.

It is also about being there, listening, thinking, asking questions and joining in.

🎒📚

Schule

When your teacher is talking, listen carefully.

When the class is doing something, join in.

👏 Mitmachen!

Sometimes your mind may travel to another world. That's okay.

But when you are in class, bring your attention back.

Look. Listen. Think. Participate.

From Mum & Dad

Dear Aroosh ❤️

We want you to remember a few simple things as you grow.

First: take care of yourself. Your life is precious.

If you are late, don't rush across a road. Stop. Look. Be safe. Life is more important than time.

If something is too high, don't jump. If you see a chemical, medicine or poison, don't touch it. Ask an adult.

We also want you to learn. At school, listen. Join in. Be a Mitmacher. Try your best.

You don't have to be the smartest person in the room. You don't have to win every game. You don't have to know everything.

Just keep learning. Keep trying. Keep being kind.

And with football... shoot your fast shots, make your big saves, help your team, and enjoy yourself.

Love yourself.
Love life.
Keep learning.
Keep playing.
Keep going.

And whenever we are strict about being on time, sleeping, listening or being careful, remember that it comes from love.

We are learning life together with you.
